What we build

AI tools built around your data and your customers.

Every project starts from a concrete business question. Our tools work on real, messy data and are built to keep AI costs under control.

Natural-language data interfaces

Let people ask questions in plain English and get answers straight from your databases: text-to-SQL, chatbots for data, and data agents that run multi-step analyses. Designed to be reliable and auditable on your own schema.

For example: A chat interface on your warehouse that answers with a query, a chart, and an explanation.

Multimodal analytics

Ask questions across photos, documents, and tables in one place. We build a working prototype on your own data, so you can judge the value before committing to a full build.

For example: Search property listings by what is in the photos and what is in the description.

Metadata enrichment and discovery

Turn unstructured images, PDFs, and product catalogs into clean, structured data with tags and attributes generated at scale. Then build search and matching interfaces on top, designed around how your customers actually look for things.

For example: Extract style and material attributes, then help customers find furniture pieces that go together.

Consulting

Already running AI on your data? We help you cut the bill.

LLM-powered SQL, chatbots, agents, and data pipelines can get expensive fast. We review your existing setup, on platforms such as Snowflake Cortex, BigQuery, or Databricks, and recommend concrete changes that reduce cost without sacrificing results.

About

Work directly with the expert.

AI-First Analytics was founded by Immanuel Trummer, Associate Professor of Computer Science at Cornell University. His work sits at the intersection of data analysis and large language models: natural-language interfaces to data, AI agents that analyze it, and efficient processing of text, tables, images, and sound.

He received an NSF CAREER award and several “Best of VLDB” and “Best of SIGMOD” distinctions, and wrote Data Analysis with LLMs, now available in five languages. Clients work with him directly: senior, hands-on engineering, with no layers of account managers.
